🏺 The Broken Vase

You are a toddler, and you just broke mom's favorite vase. To teach you a lesson, mom gives you a task: toss a coin 50 times and write down H (heads) or T (tails) each time.

After a few tosses, mom goes off to do chores. You get bored, and you start just writing H and T "randomly" yourself, from your head.

Mom comes back, looks at your list, and says: "You cheated." How did she know?

Your task

  1. On paper (or in your head), create two sequences of 50 flips:
    • Real: toss a real coin 50 times (or use a random generator, such as the button below).
    • Made up: write 50 H/T that look random, from your head, without a coin.
  2. Submit only ONE of them. Don't tell your instructor, or your neighbors, which one.
  3. Your instructor will try to guess which are real and which are made up.
